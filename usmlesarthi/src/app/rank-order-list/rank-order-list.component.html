Rank Ordered List

Instructions

Welcome to Rank order List tool, SARAL

SARAL The Sarthi ROL tool will help you identify and quantify the factors on which you can base your Rank Order List. Based on your responses, our panel can give feedback to help you rank the programs that are the best fit for you.

Before you start
  • The panelist(s) will only review the programs which are on this ROL list. So make sure this list is complete.
  • If you entered your programs in the ‘Interview Insights’, these will be automatically transferred to the ROL list.You will need to manually enter all programs into the Interview Insight Tab. (see instructions below if needed)
  • If you are a Platinum student, you will be able to discuss the list with our mentor. For other students, depending on their needs, we will hold separate sessions.
Getting Started

After logging in, go to the ROL factors tab. You will see a list of pre-populated factors. These are based on experience of other Sarthi students from previous seasons. You can add other factors unique to you as well, by clicking the “Add more ROL factors”.
You will then assign weights to these factors.

  • The total for all factors should be 100.
  • This one-time step will quantify the overall importance of the factors to you. These weights will not change for each program. You can come back and edit these factors later if needed.
  • If any pre-populated factor is not important or applicable to you, you can leave it blank or put zero. It is important to assign a reason for your distribution. This will help you analyze the factors and help our panelists understand your way of thinking.
  • Once you have completed 100/100 ranking (including adding any unique features to you), click “Save & Continue.”
Scoring the programs

Now you are ready to assign scores to the programs.

  • You will score the same set of factors that you identified earlier and score each of these for a specific program- on a scale of 1 to 10.
  • For example, let’s say you had identified ‘Type of Visa Offered’ as a factor and assigned it a weight of 60 (out of total 100). This would mean that Visa is quite an important factor for you (let’s say you prefer H1B). Now if Program A offers H1B, you should score the ‘Type of visa offered’ for this program as a 10 or some high score- since they offer the visa you want.
  • Score all factors for a program. Don’t forget to add a reason for your choice so that we can better understand your needs/wants.
  • Each factor for a program is scored independently. The total does not need to add up to 100 or any total..
  • Complete scoring for all programs.
Adding programs to the ROL list

If any program is missing from your ROL list it means it was not added to your Interview Insights database. Go to the ‘Interview Insights’ tab.

  • If the Program is listed on your dashboard, but you have not entered the experience, please provide the experience now.
  • If the program is not listed on your dashboard, this means the program was not reported. You have to enter the program now. For the interview date, select the current date if the interview is in the future. This will allow you to submit your interview experience. If the interview is yet to happen, you can’t put the interview experience and therefore it will not show up in the ROL.
  • Once you have added all your programs and experiences, go back to the ROL Order List tab- you should then be able to see the programs on your dashboard and you can continue to rank each of them.

You are now ready to generate your Rank Order List! Scroll to the bottom of the page and click “Get Rank Ordered List” to see all programs in order of ranking or “Get Summary” to see each feature you ranked for each program. .
