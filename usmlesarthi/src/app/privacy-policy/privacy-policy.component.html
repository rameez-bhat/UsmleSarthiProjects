Privacy Policy – WhatsApp API

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you interact with our services through WhatsApp. We are committed to safeguarding your data and being transparent about how it is used.

1. Information We Collect
  • Phone Number: Required to send and receive messages via WhatsApp API.
  • Message Content: Only processed for delivery; we do not share or sell this data.
  • User Metadata: Such as time, date, and delivery status of messages.
2. How We Use Your Information
  • To deliver messages and provide support through WhatsApp.
  • To improve our communication services.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely. We do not permanently store the content of messages after they are delivered, except where required by law. Industry-standard encryption and security measures are applied.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or government authorities.
  • With WhatsApp/Meta for the purpose of delivering messages via the WhatsApp Business API.
5. User Consent

We only send messages to users who have provided explicit opt-in consent. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP or contacting us directly.

6. Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting our support team.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via WhatsApp or our website.

Note: By communicating with us via WhatsApp, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
